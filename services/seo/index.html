SEO analysis & optimisation

What’s included

We start with an audit that shows exactly why your site is or is not ranking, then fix the issues in order of impact. No guaranteed rankings and no spammy links, just the work search engines reward.

Discuss your project

SEO analysis

  • Technical audit: speed, mobile usability, indexing and crawl errors
  • Keyword research based on what your customers actually search for
  • Competitor analysis: who ranks above you, and why
  • On-page review of titles, headings, descriptions and content
  • Backlink profile review
  • Google Search Console and Analytics review

Optimisation

  • Google Business Profile setup and optimisation for Maps results
  • Local SEO: consistent name, address and phone across directories
  • Structured data and technical fixes
  • Service and location pages written for real searches
  • A simple process for collecting genuine customer reviews
  • Monthly reporting on rankings, traffic and enquiries

What you get

Written audit with a prioritised fix list, keyword plan, implementation, monthly report
