Meta ads in Pollachi

What’s included

Boosting a post is not a strategy. We plan each campaign around one goal — calls, WhatsApp messages, form leads or store visits — target the people most likely to act, and report on what the spend brought in.

Discuss your project

What we run

  • Lead generation campaigns with Meta instant forms
  • Click-to-WhatsApp and Messenger campaigns
  • Local awareness campaigns around your area
  • Retargeting for website visitors and past enquiries
  • Catalogue and sales campaigns for online stores
  • Festival and seasonal offer campaigns

How we run them

  • Meta Pixel and Conversions API set up, so results are measured
  • Audience targeting by location, interests and behaviour
  • Ad creatives: images, carousels and short videos
  • Testing of creatives and audiences, with budget moved to what works
  • Monthly report on spend, leads and cost per lead

What you get

Campaign plan, ad creatives, tracking setup, ongoing optimisation, monthly report
