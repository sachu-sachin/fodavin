Custom software development

When off-the-shelf software forces your team to work around it, we build the thing that works the way you already do. Web, desktop and enterprise systems, designed around your actual process rather than a vendor’s idea of it.

Discuss a custom project

What we build

  • Internal tools and admin panels that replace spreadsheet workflows
  • Enterprise systems with role-based access and full audit trails
  • Desktop applications for Windows and macOS
  • Business process automation and scheduled jobs
  • Integrations between systems that were never meant to talk to each other
  • Reporting and analytics dashboards built on your live data

How we work

  • Discovery sessions with the people who will actually use it
  • A working prototype before full build, so you can course-correct early
  • Incremental releases rather than one big launch
  • Documentation and handover training for your team

What you get

Technical specification, working prototype, production system, source code, documentation

Web development

Sites and platforms built from scratch — no bloated page builders, no themes stretched past their limit. Fast, responsive, structured so your team can update content without calling us every time.

Discuss a web project

What we build

  • Corporate and brochure websites with a CMS your team can actually use
  • Customer and partner portals with secure logins
  • SaaS platforms, from MVP through to multi-tenant product
  • E-commerce stores with payment gateway and logistics integration
  • Landing pages built and instrumented for campaigns
  • Multilingual sites, including Indian language support

Built in from the start

  • Responsive layouts tested on real devices, not just a browser resize

  • Analytics and conversion tracking wired up at launch

Design files, responsive build, CMS setup, SEO groundwork, analytics, training

UI/UX design

Design driven by how people actually behave, not by what looks good in a portfolio shot. Every element earns its place, and the ones that do not get cut before they cost you development time.

Discuss a ui/ux project

Research and structure

  • User interviews and stakeholder workshops
  • Competitor and heuristic review of what you have now
  • Information architecture and user flows
  • Wireframes, from rough sketches to annotated screens

Interface and system

  • High-fidelity interface design for web and mobile
  • Clickable prototypes you can put in front of real users
  • A design system with reusable components and tokens
  • Accessibility review: contrast, focus states, keyboard paths
  • Design-to-development handover with specs developers can build from

What you get

Research findings, wireframes, prototypes, final UI, design system

FigmaDesign tokensWCAG AA

Keeping it moving
