Overview

Prime Real Estate sells design-led homes. Listing sites usually flatten every property into the same grey grid, so we built the experience around the architecture itself: full-bleed hero photography, a search bar that gets out of the way, and featured projects presented as a curated collection.

The dark, gold-accented identity carries through listings, insights and the consultation flow, so the brand does not stop at the homepage.

The challenge

Buyers browse property emotionally and then filter ruthlessly. The site had to do both — let someone fall for a house on the homepage, then narrow a growing catalogue by location, type, price and bedrooms without ever feeling like a spreadsheet.

What we built

  • Cinematic homepage with a persistent location, type, price and bedroom search
  • Featured properties collection with location, pricing and specification cards
  • Listing detail pages with galleries, specifications and neighbourhood context
  • Credibility strip covering experience, completed projects, satisfaction and awards
  • Consultation booking with enquiry routing to the right agent
  • Insights and testimonials sections built for long-tail search
  • Listing CMS so the team can publish properties without a developer
