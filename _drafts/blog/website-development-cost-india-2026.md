# Website Development Cost in India 2026

The honest answer to "how much does a website cost in India?" is that it depends on what the website has to do. A five-page site for a local business and an online store with payments, delivery and stock are different projects, and their prices reflect that. This guide breaks down what goes into the cost, gives typical ranges, and lists the running costs most quotes leave out, so you can compare quotes properly.

## What decides the cost of a website
- Number of pages and content
- Custom design vs template
- Features: forms, booking, payments, logins, integrations
- Who writes the content and supplies photos
- Ongoing maintenance (AMC)
