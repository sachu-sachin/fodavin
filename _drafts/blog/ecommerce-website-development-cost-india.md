# Ecommerce Website Development Cost in India

An online store has more moving parts than a business website: a catalogue, a cart, a payment gateway, delivery rules, invoices and an admin panel your team uses every day. That is why ecommerce website development cost in India varies so much between quotes. This post explains where the money goes, compares ready platforms with custom builds, and lists the running costs that keep a store working after launch.

## What affects the cost of an ecommerce website
- Number of products and variants
- Ready platform (Shopify, WooCommerce) vs custom build
- Payment, shipping and GST invoicing integrations
- Integration with billing or inventory software
