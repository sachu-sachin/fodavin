Billing & ERP Software

01
Overview

Billing software for small business in India, built to fit

We build billing software for small businesses that have outgrown manual bills or a tool that almost fits. Invoices, stock, purchases, customers, credit and reports sit in one system, set up around your counters, godowns and the way your staff work.

When billing is not enough, the same system grows into an ERP. Our team works from Pollachi and builds billing and ERP software for businesses across Tamil Nadu and India.

What’s included in our billing and ERP software

Start with billing, then add the ERP modules you need as you grow.

When it fits

Do you need custom billing software?

If one or more of these sounds familiar, it is worth a conversation.

Bills are written or typed twice

Entries are repeated in books, spreadsheets and accounts.

Stock never matches

What the system says and what is on the shelf are different.

You have more than one branch

Each location keeps its own records, and totals come late.

Off-the-shelf software does not fit

Your pricing, schemes or workflow need workarounds.
