About us

Fodavin.

Fodavin Technologies is an IT and digital marketing company based in Pollachi, Tamil Nadu. We help businesses get online, get found and win more customers: we build the website, app or software, then bring people to it with SEO, Meta ads and video.

Many businesses end up juggling a web developer, an ads freelancer and a video editor who never talk to each other. We put all of that under one roof, so the site, the ads and the content are planned together and measured against the same goal: enquiries and sales. Our office is at Sree Saraswathi Thyagaraja College (STC) on Palani Road, so you can meet the team in person.
