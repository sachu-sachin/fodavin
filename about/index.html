About Fodavin – IT Company in Pollachi

Fodavin.

Fodavin Technologies is an IT and digital marketing company based in Pollachi, Tamil Nadu. We help businesses get online, get found and win more customers: we build the website, app or software, then bring people to it with SEO, Meta ads and video.

Many businesses end up juggling a web developer, an ads freelancer and a video editor who never talk to each other. We put all of that under one roof, so the site, the ads and the content are planned together and measured against the same goal: enquiries and sales. Our office is at Sree Saraswathi Thyagaraja College (STC) on Palani Road, so you can meet the team in person.

Who we are

An IT company in Pollachi, working across India

Fodavin was founded in 2024 by a group of young friends who had worked at MNCs and startups, as developers and in management. They wanted to bring that experience home, and to give local businesses the same quality of work that large companies get. Today we are a team of 10 people.

Fodavin Technologies is an IT company in Pollachi that builds websites, ecommerce stores, mobile apps and custom software, and then brings customers to them through SEO, Google Business Profile, social media, Meta ads and video editing.

Most of our clients are businesses in Pollachi and nearby towns, but the work itself can happen anywhere. We work with businesses in Pollachi, Coimbatore, Tiruppur, Udumalpet and across India. We meet clients in Tamil or English, in person or online, whichever suits them, and we keep the same team on your project from the first call to the monthly report.
